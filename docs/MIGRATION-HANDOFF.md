## Confirmed design

- **Sequence and learning.** Chapter 1 has 21 untimed mains. C1-01 to C1-03 are straight-to-marker jobs, C1-04 to C1-08 teach and practice steering and braking, C1-09 to C1-13 cover single-package pickup and escape, C1-14 to C1-18 cover one-patrol deliveries, and C1-19 to C1-21 revisit and combine learned rules. C1-01 starts aligned and clears by automatic acceleration. Practice varies routes and placements under the same mechanics, hazards, restrictions, and failure conditions. Later chapters and their counts remain open. [Exact approved order](../HEATWAVE-GETAWAY-DESIGN.md#first-playable-chapter-1) · [Authored missions](../src/content/chapter1.json)
- **World and story.** Author one shared map. Use portions for missions and extend it as concrete spatial needs arise. District themes guide later work. Ray drives and Nina gives missions. Story owns narrative, dialogue, and flavour after gameplay rules and sequence are settled. Current dialogue is placeholder copy.
- **Screens and visual direction.** Story is Home. The full-bleed Palmera Bay map has a winding dotted path, numbered nodes, district unlocks on the path, bottom navigation, and seamless vertical wrap. One pre-job postcard uses Nina’s speech bubble and supports a portrait layout. The Chapter 1 teaching inset appears on C1-01, C1-04, C1-09, and C1-14. Play and eligible car selection sit beneath the artwork. Driving fills the viewport with icon-led objective, damage, and five-star heat indicators. Pause overlays driving. The Ocean Drive UI palette uses midnight blue `#101C3A`, aqua `#34E7E1`, hot pink `#FF4FA7`, and Sunset Motel marigold `#FFD368`. Pink and marigold are prominent on story and instruction screens. The Canal Ward gameplay art reference governs its compact 3D geometry, consistent scale, weathered materials, and localized amber lighting over wet charcoal and teal. The accepted opening art is Nina pointing at an envelope. Detailed postcard layout and Garage visuals remain open.
- **Results and saves.** Results and bonuses reuse the postcard structure. Failure art varies by cause. Continue to Story banks the haul immediately. Bonus opens an optional briefing with Start Bonus and Back to Success. Starting the bonus risks the unbanked haul and carries over damage. Success auto-banks. Failure loses the haul and preserves the main clear. The foundation preserves a pending clear and haul after reload and banks each result once.
- **Vehicles.** Theft missions lock the specified vehicle. Where selection is permitted, the mission starts with its default and allows owned alternatives of the same category that meet the district tier. Categories are hatchback, coupe, supercar, van, and truck. Truck covers pickups, off-road models, and heavy box or tow models. Canal Ward, Neon Strip, and Breakwater Port use tiers 1, 2, and 3 respectively.
- **Campaign objective families.** Collect and escape, delivery, survive pursuit, beat the time, ram and collect, tailing, route manipulation, racing against other cars in sprint or circuit, ram and destroy, and catch another car. The ten families define the design for the wider campaign. Escort missions are out of scope. Vehicle swaps, cargo capacity, and towing support objectives. [Working design](../HEATWAVE-GETAWAY-DESIGN.md#mission-objectives)
